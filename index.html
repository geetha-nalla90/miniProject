About Us

LifeDrop is a modern blood donation platform dedicated to connecting blood seekers with willing donors in real-time. Recognizing the critical delay in emergencies, we use location-based matching to instantly notify nearby eligible donors. Our goal is to create a seamless, transparent, and prompt healthcare communication system that eliminates the stressful friction during emergency blood requirements.
